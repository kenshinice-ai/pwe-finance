Investment Loan Strategies

Negative Gearing

Tax outcomes should be discussed with your accountant. From a lending perspective, we help compare structures that support cash flow, repayment comfort and future borrowing plans.

Capital Growth Strategy

We focus on finance structure rather than property advice: deposit source, equity use, loan features and repayment type all affect how comfortably you can hold an investment.

Offset accounts, redraw facilities and split loans can affect cash flow and flexibility. We explain the feature trade-offs before you apply.
